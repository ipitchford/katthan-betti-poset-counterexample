# Status

Unrefereed research candidate, version 2.0.0-candidate, 14 October 2026.

The ideal assertion of Katthän's Betti-poset conjecture is refuted by a six-generator example over every field. The finite five-atom census supports the sharp generator threshold. The quotient assertion remains open; this example satisfies it. Stanley's inequality for ideals and the restricted stoss programme are not refuted.

Written proof, finite certificate checking, exhaustive census, package integrity and public availability are distinct evidence classes. There is no claim of formal verification or external editorial peer review.
