# Source-to-claim audit

14 October 2026, internal publication assessment. Primary source inspection is bounded to the arguments listed below, not an assertion that every cited paper was read in full.

- Katthän, *Betti posets and the Stanley depth*, [arXiv:1509.08275v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.08275v2), [published version](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40598-016-0039-5): Sections 2–3, the distinct ideal/quotient assertions, meet closure including ambient top, squarefree colon formulation, and restricted stoss programme. Neither ideal failure nor the present colon realization resolves the quotient assertion. The tensor-product remark supplies an inequality, not the additivity needed for automatic amplification.
- Ichim–Katthän–Moyano-Fernández, *Stanley depth and the lcm-lattice*, [arXiv:1405.3602](https://arxiv.org/html/1405.3602): Theorem 4.5, Lemmas 4.3–4.4, Proposition 5.2 and its proof. The surjective join map requires a singleton bottom fibre. The generator bound is attributed there to Okazaki, not to the characteristic-poset theorem.
- Ichim–Katthän–Moyano-Fernández, *LCM-lattices and Stanley depth: A first computational approach*, [arXiv:1408.4255](https://arxiv.org/html/1408.4255): Theorem 1.2, Algorithm 3.1 and deletion-generation justification. The at-most-five-generator quotient result is known. The release's new comparison concerns the ideal invariant across Betti-poset classes. Census completeness uses Boolean inclusion and closure under allowable deletions, not merely a matching total count.
- Herzog–Vladoiu–Zheng, *How to compute the Stanley depth of a monomial ideal*, DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2008.01.006: the characteristic-poset framework is an external mathematical dependency. The revised manuscript explicitly proves the normalized-interval refinement used by its finite encoding; it does not claim a proof-assistant verification of the source theorem.
- Gasharov–Peeva–Welker, DOI 10.4310/MRL.1999.v6.n5.a5: the lcm-lattice homology formula is an external mathematical dependency. The release checks the specific integral crosscut complexes; bottom beta_0 is handled separately.

Bounded refreshed searches for the precise Katthän assertion and ideal/quotient counterexamples found no matching earlier resolution. Absence from those searches is not exhaustive priority certification. Cited primary texts and private referee material are not redistributed.
